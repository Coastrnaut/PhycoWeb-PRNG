## How to answer

- Reply "all recommended", or give letters by ID, for example "SEC-010 B, HW-003 C, the rest
  recommended". An option may also be answered in your own words.
- After the answer, the changes each chosen option names are applied with their reasons. The
  six checklists are then re-read against the changed text, and the reviewer ticks an item once
  the text it asks for is in place.
- IDs: `SEC`, `RAT`, `ANA`, `PRO`, `HW` and `REP` stand for the checklists
  [security.md](./checklists/security.md),
  [design-rationale.md](./checklists/design-rationale.md),
  [analysis.md](./checklists/analysis.md), [protocol.md](./checklists/protocol.md),
  [hardware.md](./checklists/hardware.md) and
  [reproducibility.md](./checklists/reproducibility.md). `SEC-010` is CHK010 of the security
  checklist.
- The first word of each recommendation says what kind of change it is:

  | Word | Meaning |
  |------|---------|
  | Clarify | States what the documents already mean; no rule changes |
  | Align | Removes a disagreement between two texts, keeping the stricter or more careful one |
  | Add | A new requirement, record, test or check outside the protocol's decision rules |
  | Stricter | Tightens a rule of the pre-registered protocol in the direction FR-044 allows; the row names the direction |
  | Settle | Fixes a case the protocol leaves open, by its most conservative reading, before any result it affects (PRO-027) |

- From plan approval the protocol may only become stricter (FR-044), and no result exists yet.
  No recommended option loosens it. An alternative that would is marked "looser", so the choice
  is visible but cannot be taken without breaking FR-044.
- The "Changes" column names where the recommended option would be written. "spec FR-006" is a
  requirement of [spec.md](./spec.md), "research R14" a section of [research.md](./research.md),
  and "data-model", "model contract", "cli contract", "plan" and "quickstart" are
  [data-model.md](./data-model.md), [contracts/model-v3-api.md](./contracts/model-v3-api.md),
  [contracts/cli.md](./contracts/cli.md), [plan.md](./plan.md) and
  [quickstart.md](./quickstart.md). "security.md" is the repository's
  [security.md](../../security.md).
- Some rows lean on others; they say so by ID. Two pairs matter most: HW-003 and RAT-019 define
  a round and a stage together, and HW-010 changes the key binding that SEC-038 and HW-014
  build on.
